# EMBER — Immersive Demo Video Script

> **Target Duration:** ~2 Minutes
> **Vibe:** Atmospheric, game-like, HUD-technical. Think *Journey* meets a data-scientist's terminal. No traditional web app navigation bars or separate "pages." The entire experience happens within one continuous, living spatial environment.

---

### **[0:00 – 0:30] ACT I: The Descent (Immersion)**

**[VISUAL]**
*Fade in from black. No menus. No web navigation.* 
*We are immediately inside the 700vh parallax space. It feels massive, dark, and atmospheric. Glowing geometric shapes (past entries) float in the mid-ground and background, drifting slightly.*
*The user scrolls. Instead of a webpage moving up, the camera physically dives *forward* and *downward* through the 3D space, passing by glowing amber and deep violet structures.*
*A sleek, minimalist HUD (monospace font, thin leader lines) occasionally flickers data when passing near an object.*

**[VOICEOVER]**
"This is Ember. It’s not a dashboard. It’s a spatial environment for your mind. When you open Ember, you don't see a blank page—you see the living architecture of your emotional history, existing in a persistent, continuous void."

---

### **[0:30 – 1:00] ACT II: Diegetic Logging & Real-Time Genesis**

**[VISUAL]**
*The camera stops moving. The user hits the `Spacebar`.*
*The environment blurs slightly. A crisp, terminal-like text cursor blinks into existence right in the center of the void.*
*Live typing:* `"I felt completely frozen today. I couldn't get any work done and just stared at the screen."*
*The user hits `Enter`.*
*Instantly, the text dissolves into a wireframe geometric shape. We watch the Qwen AI process it in real-time: the shape rapidly pulses through colors before solidifying into a heavy, sharp, deep violet crystal. It physically drops into the environment, shifting the surrounding objects slightly.*

**[VOICEOVER]**
"To log a thought, you simply type into the void. 

There are no forms. When you finish, Ember’s AI analyzes the raw emotional intensity of your words in real-time. You don't wait for a response; you watch your thought physically materialize into the world. Heavy, anxious days crystallize into dense, dark violets, while lighter moments bloom into warm ambers."

---

### **[1:00 – 1:30] ACT III: The Constellation & The Deep Void**

**[VISUAL]**
*The user zooms out, showing the massive, sweeping mathematical arc of all their past shapes—a literal constellation of their mental state.*
*The user performs a rapid, deep scroll, plunging to the absolute bottom of the environment.*
*The lighting gets darker. At the very bottom rests a massive, slowly rotating object.*
*The HUD locks onto it, projecting thin technical lines reading: `INTENSITY MAX / CORE MEMORY`.*

**[VOICEOVER]**
"Because this is a spatial world, you can actually explore your emotional rhythm. You can zoom out to see the overarching patterns of your weeks. 

And at the deepest layer of the environment lies The Deep Void. Ember naturally pulls your most intense, heaviest memories to the foundation of the world. It gives you a dedicated, quiet space to confront and process your hardest moments without them getting lost in a feed."

---

### **[1:30 – 2:00] ACT IV: The Bridge (Extraction)**

**[VISUAL]**
*The user clicks on the massive object in the Deep Void. The camera snaps to it.*
*A clean, technical HUD overlay expands from the object, showing its sentiment breakdown.*
*The user clicks a minimalist icon labeled `[ EXTRACT DIAGNOSTICS ]`.*
*A clean data packet is generated on-screen, ready to be shared.*
*Fade to black.*

**[VOICEOVER]**
"Ember never tries to give you advice. It is strictly an analytical mirror. 

But when you are ready to break the isolation, you can extract a clinical, data-driven diagnostic of your environment to share directly with a therapist or a trusted friend. 

This is Ember. A world for your mind."
