# Ember — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

> **Target Duration:** ~2 Minutes  
> **Pacing:** Calm, measured, narrative-driven  
> **Key Themes:** Visual feedback loop, distraction-free reflection, responsible AI, emotional shift proof.

---

### **[0:00 – 0:20] ACT I: The Hook & The Problem**

**[VISUAL]**  
*Full screen recording of a blank text box in a generic journal app, or starting on Ember’s minimalist homepage. Slow zoom in.*

**[VOICEOVER]**  
"Journaling is one of the most effective tools for mental health. Therapists recommend it, research backs it—and yet, most people quit within weeks. 

The reason is simple: you open an app, type into a blank box, close the app, and nothing happens. There's no feedback, no reward, no reason to return tomorrow. Most apps try to fix this with streak counters and notification badges that exploit guilt. 

We built **Ember** to change that."

---

### **[0:20 – 0:50] ACT II: The Core Feedback Loop**

**[VISUAL]**  
*Cut to clicking "Log an Entry". Show the full-screen, distraction-free writing space.*  
*Type a real, honest entry live:* `"Felt overwhelmed by deadlines today, but took a 20-minute walk outside and finally breathed."`  
*Click Submit. Watch the instant transition back to the spatial world as the new memory ember illuminates.*

**[VOICEOVER]**  
"Ember gives you something back immediately. 

When you write in Ember's dedicated, distraction-free logging space, you don't write into a void. Every entry is analyzed by Alibaba’s Qwen AI for emotional intensity and sentiment. 

Rather than generating unsolicited advice, the AI translates your words directly into dynamic visual properties—shaping a living, persistent world that evolves with every honest thought."

---

### **[0:50 – 1:20] ACT III: Spatial Arc & Dual Zones**

**[VISUAL]**  
*Slowly scroll through the 700vh parallax landscape. Show the glowing cards sweeping along the arc.*  
*Hover over warm amber cards (light days) and deep violet cards (heavy days).*

**[VOICEOVER]**  
"Your entries become glowing cards arranged along a sweeping mathematical arc. 

Lighter days blossom in warm ambers and golds; heavier days take root in deep, cool violets. 

As your archive grows, scrolling through your world becomes a deeply comforting experience. You aren't staring at flat logs or red streak marks—you are looking at visual proof that heavy periods end, and lighter days follow dark ones."

---

### **[1:20 – 1:45] ACT IV: Insights & The Deep Void**

**[VISUAL]**  
1. *Click over to the `/insights` page. Show the clean Emotional Topography breakdown.*  
2. *Scroll all the way down to the bottom of the main parallax landscape to reveal **The Deep Void**.*

**[VOICEOVER]**  
"On the **Emotional Topography** page, Ember maps the subtle shifts between your positive and negative zones over time. 

And at the deepest point of your scroll journey lies **The Deep Void**—where your most emotionally intense experience is surfaced, giving you a dedicated space to revisit and process your most significant moments."

---

### **[1:45 – 2:00] ACT V: Responsible AI & Closing**

**[VISUAL]**  
*Zoom out to the full glowing parallax landscape. Slow tilt up as the ambient light shimmers.*

**[VOICEOVER]**  
"Importantly, Ember’s AI is strictly analytical. It scores sentiment to render visuals—it never attempts to act as a chatbot therapist or deliver unsupervised medical advice. 

Streaks and badges exploit guilt. Ember rewards the act of reflection itself. 

Thank you for experiencing Ember."

---

## 🎬 Recording Checklist & Tips

1. **Screen Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps.
2. **Audio Setup:** Clear microphone, quiet room, calm speaking pace.
3. **Mouse Movement:** Slow, deliberate cursor movements during parallax scrolling.
4. **Lighting & Contrast:** Keep your monitor at full brightness to let the glowing amber and violet gradients pop.
